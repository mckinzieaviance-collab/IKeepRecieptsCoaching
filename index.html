Authoritative Vocabulary: Erasing "I Feel"

Replace tentative corporate language with authoritative executive presence. Stop sounding apologetic or uncertain in writing and spoken meetings.

• Instead of "I feel like maybe we should..." → "I recommend..."

• Instead of "Does this look okay?" → "I will proceed with this plan by 3 PM unless you advise otherwise."
